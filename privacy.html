Section 01

1. Introduction

Welcome to the official Privacy Policy of Siya Infotech & Solar Energy (“Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, handling, processing, protection, and disclosure of personal, professional, and operational information gathered when authorized employees, contractors, field technicians, sales representatives, and administrators utilize the Siya Infotech & Solar Energy Staff App (the “Application” or “App”).

We are committed to maintaining the privacy and trust of our workforce and customers. We comply with applicable Indian information technology and data protection regulations, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

Important Enterprise Clarification This Application is strictly an internal enterprise productivity and project management tool restricted to authorized staff members and solar contractors of Siya Infotech & Solar Energy. It is not a public commercial marketplace and does not offer open self-registration without administrator verification.
Section 02

2. Application Scope & Architecture

The Siya Infotech & Solar Energy Staff App functions as the centralized operational engine for our solar engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) business. The primary features of the Application include:

  • Staff Authentication & Profile Management: Secure role-based login for engineers, surveyors, installation teams, and back-office staff.
  • GPS-Based Attendance Check-in / Check-out: Momentary location verification during daily punch-in, punch-out, and on-site job arrival.
  • Customer Lifecycle Management: Onboarding prospective solar consumers, recording electricity sanction data, and tracking DISCOM applications.
  • Solar Engineering Feasibility & Project Execution: Recording rooftop dimensions, shadow surveys, structure fabrication, inverter mounting, and DC/AC electrical wiring milestones.
  • Site Photography & Work Completion Reports (WCR): Uploading installation proof photographs for MSEDCL and PM Surya Ghar subsidy disbursement.
  • Document & Stamp Paper Agreement Uploads: Digital archiving of electricity bills, tax receipts, Aadhaar cards, and Consumer-Vendor agreements.
  • Task Assignment & Push Notifications: Assigning survey and installation tickets to field teams with real-time push alerts.
  • Payment & Sub-Vendor Settlement Tracking: Recording customer margin money receipts, loan sanction statuses, and contractor payout ledgers.
Section 03

3. Information We Collect

In order to fulfill business obligations, execute rooftop solar installations, and verify employee work milestones, the Application processes specific categories of professional and operational data:

3.1 Staff Account Information

When an employee account is provisioned by the administrator, we store the staff member’s full legal name, assigned employee code (e.g., SIYA-EMP-042), registered official email address, mobile phone number, job role / designation (e.g., Solar Technician, Site Engineer, Sales Manager), and assigned operational branch or territory. Passwords are never stored in plain text and are protected using industry-standard irreversible salted hashing (bcrypt) via our secure Supabase Auth infrastructure.

3.2 GPS Attendance Information (Geofenced Check-in / Check-out)

When a staff member performs a daily attendance check-in, check-out, or logs arrival at a customer installation site, the Application accesses the device’s precise or approximate GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, accuracy radius, and server timestamp).

Strict Foreground Attendance Only — No Continuous Background Tracking GPS location is requested strictly on-demand at the precise instant the staff member taps the “Check In”, “Check Out”, or “Mark Site Arrival” button. We do NOT track, record, monitor, or broadcast your continuous location in the background when the Application is closed or inactive. Once the punch-in record is saved, GPS hardware access is immediately terminated.

3.3 Customer Information

To facilitate rooftop solar installations and secure approvals under government schemes (such as the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and state DISCOM portals like MSEDCL), staff members capture customer details upon verbal/written consent:

Customer Identity

Customer Name, mobile phone number, secondary contact, premises address, city, district, and pin code.

DISCOM Technical Data

Consumer Number (Consumer ID/BU), Tariff category (LT-Residential/Commercial), Sanctioned Load (kW), Billing history.

Site & Solar Parameters

Proposed Solar Capacity (e.g. 3 kW, 5 kW), Phase type (Single Phase / 3 Phase), Roof surface type (RCC, Tin shade), Shadow survey details.

Subsidy & Sanction Tracking

Application submission numbers, DISCOM load sanction letter reference, meter testing report ID, and commissioning dates.

3.4 Camera and Site Photographs

The Application utilizes the device camera and media gallery with explicit user authorization to document physical solar installations:

  • Pre-installation site feasibility photos (rooftop area, south direction orientation, shadow obstructions).
  • Existing electricity meter reading and consumer display board photos.
  • Structure fabrication, foundation anchor bolting, and solar panel mounting verification photos.
  • Inverter mounting, DC Distribution Box (DCDB), AC Distribution Box (ACDB), and copper earthing pit photos.
  • Final installed solar plant, net meter display, and joint physical inspection photographs.

The camera is never accessed covertly or without the user directly initiating a photo capture action inside the App interface.

3.5 Documents and File Uploads

To complete statutory documentation and financial agreements, staff members scan or upload required PDF and image documents:

  • Recent electricity bills for load verification.
  • Proof of premises ownership (property tax receipt, 7/12 extract, or index-II document).
  • Customer identity proof (Aadhaar card copy — masked format recommended).
  • Bank passbook copy or cancelled cheque (required for direct subsidy bank transfer by central/state nodal agencies).
  • Signed physical or e-stamp Consumer-Vendor agreements, Form X (Transfer of Security Deposit), and Self-Undertaking affidavits.

3.6 Task Management & Financial Tracking Records

The Application logs operational workflow data including: task assignment details, ticket status updates, sub-vendor material outward dispatches (solar panels, inverters, cables issued from warehouse inventory), installation completion timestamps, payment receipt reference numbers (NEFT, UPI UTR numbers, or Cheque details), and contractor settlement ledgers.

Note on Payments: The Application only records administrative accounting reference records. We do not store or process debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or NetBanking transaction passwords.

Section 04

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through the Application strictly for genuine business execution, employee management, and legal compliance. Specifically:

  1. Attendance Verification & Payroll: Calculating staff work hours, verifying attendance location authenticity, and processing monthly salary credits.
  2. Solar Feasibility & System Design: Computing optimum rooftop solar kilowatt capacity, string sizing, inverter compatibility, and projected annual energy generation.
  3. Statutory DISCOM & National Portal Filings: Assisting customers in filing net metering applications on the National Portal for Rooftop Solar (PM Surya Ghar) and MSEDCL portals for grid connectivity and government subsidy disbursement.
  4. Work Completion Reporting (WCR): Compiling photographic dossiers and engineering reports required by power utility inspectors prior to bi-directional net meter synchronization.
  5. Inventory & Material Tracking: Ensuring solar panels, grid-tie inverters, AC/DC protection units, and balance-of-system (BOS) materials outwarded to Sub-Vendors and contractors are accounted for date-wise.
  6. Operational Push Notifications: Transmitting real-time task alerts, urgent service tickets, approval statuses, and schedule changes to field technicians.
  7. Audit & Legal Compliance: Maintaining enterprise accounting records, warranty support logs, and statutory audit compliance under Indian tax and commercial laws.

Section 06

6. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure Policy

Zero Commercial Data Selling or Monetization Siya Infotech & Solar Energy does NOT sell, rent, lease, trade, or commercially monetize staff personal data or customer records to third-party marketing agencies, advertisers, or data brokers under any circumstances.

Data recorded in the Application is disclosed solely under the following limited enterprise conditions:

  • Government & Utility Portals: Customer identity, premises documents, and site survey photos are uploaded directly to the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), the National Portal for Rooftop Solar, and the state DISCOM (e.g., MSEDCL) with customer consent to process grid-connected net metering approvals and direct benefit transfer (DBT) subsidies.
  • Authorized Solar Sub-Vendors & Contractors: Site location, solar capacity, and material dispatch records are shared with designated installation contractors solely for physical plant erection and commissioning.
  • Enterprise Cloud Infrastructure: Data is processed via secure infrastructure providers (e.g., Supabase, PostgreSQL, Firebase Cloud Messaging) bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
  • Statutory & Law Enforcement Compliance: We may disclose records if strictly required to do so by applicable law, court order, or official governmental directive in accordance with Indian legal procedures.
Section 07

7. Data Security Standards

We employ robust technical, cryptographic, and organizational security measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or accidental destruction:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application, web management console, and database endpoints utilize Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database volumes, file attachments, and private document storage buckets are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 cryptographic algorithms.
  • Granular Row Level Security (RLS): Database tables enforce PostgreSQL Row Level Security policies, ensuring staff members can only view customer records and sites assigned to their territory or authorization tier.
  • Credential Protection: Passwords are protected using irreversible salt-and-hash functions (bcrypt). Staff sessions use short-lived JWT tokens with automatic expiration.
  • Immutable Audit Trails: Sensitive operations — such as material outward dispatch, inventory stock deduction, payment recording, and outward cancellation — generate automated audit log entries containing timestamps and user identification.
Section 08

8. Data Retention Policy

We retain information only for as long as necessary to achieve the operational purposes outlined in this policy or to satisfy statutory legal, accounting, and tax obligations:

  • Staff Account & Employment Records: Maintained for the duration of active employment plus statutory periods mandated under Indian labour and industrial employment laws.
  • GPS Attendance Records: Maintained for a rolling period of 36 months for payroll verification, employee attendance audits, and statutory labor reporting.
  • Customer Solar Project Dossiers: Solar rooftop systems have a typical operational lifespan of 25 years. Technical drawings, inverter serial numbers, warranty certificates, and Work Completion Reports are preserved for the system warranty lifecycle to provide ongoing servicing, AMC, and replacement assistance.
  • Financial & Invoicing Ledgers: Preserved for 8 financial years in strict accordance with the Companies Act, 2013 and Goods and Services Tax (GST) Act.

9. Account Deletion & Data Subject Rights

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies on User Data and DPDPA guidelines, we provide clear mechanisms for staff members and consumers to request review, correction, or deletion of their personal records:

How to Request Account Deletion

If an employee resigns, leaves the organization, or wishes to request deletion of their account credentials and personal profile, they can initiate a request through either of the following methods:

  1. Email Request: Send an email from your registered staff email address to siyainfodigital@gmail.com with the subject line “Staff Account Deletion Request - [Your Name / Employee Code]”.
  2. Administrative Portal Action: Contact the Master Administrator at the corporate office. The administrator can deactivate, block, or delete the staff account credentials via the Master Admin Panel in real time.
Timeline & Statutory Exceptions Upon receipt of a verified deletion request, we will deactivate account credentials within 7 business days and complete permanent deletion of personal profile data within 30 days. Historical tax invoices, statutory material outward ledgers, and signed legal agreements will be retained only to the minimum extent required by Indian law.
